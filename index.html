Are AURA formulations safe for sensitive or reactive skin?

Yes. All of our formulations are 100% free from synthetic fragrances, drying alcohols, parabens, and essential oil allergens. Every formulation undergoes clinical dermatologist patch testing on sensitive skin panels to guarantee barrier compatibility.

How does Ayurvedic Bakuchiol compare to traditional Retinol?

Bakuchiol stimulates cellular turnover and collagen synthesis similarly to retinol. However, it does not cause redness, peeling, or sun sensitivity, making it gentle enough for sensitive skin and safe for both morning and evening routines.

What is your shipping policy and delivery timeline?

We offer Complimentary Standard Shipping on all domestic orders over $60. Standard orders ship within 24 hours and arrive within 2–4 business days. Flat-rate $5.00 shipping applies to orders under $60.

What is your return and 30-day satisfaction guarantee?

We stand behind every bottle. If you are not completely satisfied with your botanical results within 30 days of delivery, contact our concierge team for a full refund or complimentary product exchange.

Are your bottles and shipping packaging sustainable?

Yes. We package all formulations in UV-protective amber and frosted glass bottles that are 100% infinitely recyclable. All shipping boxes are made from 100% post-consumer recycled paper and printed with non-toxic vegetable inks.

How should I layer multiple AURA serums in my ritual?

Always apply products from thinnest to thickest texture: cleanse first, apply your active serum (like Vitamin C in the AM or Bakuchiol in the PM), follow with your Ceramide Cream, and seal with 2–3 drops of Facial Squalane Oil.
